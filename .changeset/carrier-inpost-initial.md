Add `@open-mercato/carrier-inpost` — InPost shipping carrier integration for Open Mercato.

Provides rate calculation, shipment creation, cancellation, and webhook tracking for InPost locker and courier services (Poland). Includes a demo backend page, widget injection for configuration and tracking, and integration tests (TC-INPOST-001–004).
