Add `@open-mercato/forms` — audit-grade questionnaire / form primitive for Open Mercato.

Versioned form definitions (immutable once published), append-only submission revisions, role-sliced rendering, per-tenant envelope encryption, GDPR-safe anonymization, public anonymous renderer with distribution + invitation tokens, signed PDF snapshots, and a drag-and-drop visual studio with constrained custom styling (themes, section/field styles, gradient backgrounds). Imported from the open-mercato monorepo via `yarn official-modules promote forms`.
